## What it actually is

'If You Were President — 2026 Edition': a single-file, zero-dependency choice simulation grounded in real 2025–26 events. 20 scenarios (US-China trade war, Iran brinkmanship, AI job displacement, crypto reserve status…), 5 tracked stats, 16 rounds, 13 endings, with contextual blurbs citing real sources (Pew, Stanford AI Index, NATO briefings). Fork of SpaceYouLand/President-Sim (same author's org).
